España al Grano

España al Grano is a desktop tool that lets a creator upload their own videos to their own TikTok account from their computer, using an AI assistant (Claude) as the interface.

It uses TikTok Login Kit to connect the creator's account and the TikTok Content Posting API to upload videos as drafts or publish them after the creator's explicit confirmation. All credentials are stored only on the creator's own computer.

España al Grano es una herramienta de escritorio con la que un creador sube sus propios vídeos a su propia cuenta de TikTok desde su ordenador, usando un asistente de IA (Claude) como interfaz. Utiliza Login Kit y la Content Posting API oficiales de TikTok.
